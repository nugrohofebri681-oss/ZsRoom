Spec: `specs/characters/char_zila.json` · Height 165 cm (5 cm shorter than Febri; allowed range 3–6 cm) · proposed 4.6 heads · canvas 256×400 @ 2 px/cm · pivot (128, 376)

## Identity locks (must survive every outfit, facing and animation)
- Long layered **brown** hair to mid-back; **bangs** and face-framing strands; hair volume consistent across facings.
- **Two pink hair ornaments**, one on each side of the head (symmetric pair — safe to mirror).
- **Large warm-brown eyes**, soft rounded face, gentle warm expression.
- Default outfit: **pink and white** (soft pink top, white/cream bottom).

## Proportion guide (proposal)
Head ≈ 36 cm (165/4.6), the same head size as Febri. Hair volume on top must not add more than ~2 cm of visible height. Shoulders ≈ 1.3 head widths. Legs ≈ 45 % of height. Hands visible and expressive. Slightly larger eyes than realistic, but no anime "sparkle" convention and no tiny-mouth/huge-eye exaggeration.

## Required for the first character quality gate (later milestone)
Turnaround S / SE / E / NE / N (neutral pose) → one walk cycle (S facing, 8 frames) → CD review → only then the rest.
