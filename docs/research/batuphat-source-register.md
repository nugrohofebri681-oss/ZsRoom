# Batuphat — research source register (P6.1, Issue #40)

**Research date:** 2026-11-23.
**Tools:** WebSearch (server-side search) plus direct page downloads from this session's container (curl / headless Chromium through the environment proxy). The WebFetch tool could not resolve any host in this environment, so pages were downloaded directly after the environment's network allowlist was extended.

**Read status used below:**
- **READ** — the page itself was downloaded and its text read on 2026-11-23.
- **SEARCH-ONLY** — only a search engine's result summary was seen. The page itself could not be read: `rri.co.id` serves a bot-verification wall (HTTP 403) even to a real browser, and two Pemko news pages returned "upstream request failed". Treat these claims as secondary until someone opens the page in a normal browser.
- **NOT READ** — listed for completeness; nothing is claimed from it.

**Confidence:** high = official registry/document READ; medium = credible source but SEARCH-ONLY, or a single non-official source; low = blog/listing, outdated or conflicting.

> ZsRoom's town is a **compressed, fictionalised game neighbourhood (80 × 57 m)**, not a survey of Batuphat. Real places inspire building types, activities and atmosphere. In-game coordinates never claim to match real ones. Zila's home and the story places (Mixue, dimsum workplace, Indomaret position) are story locations, **not mapped to any real address**.

The two registry points are about **1.6 km apart** (straight line). Our whole town is 80 × 57 m, so real landmarks **cannot** all be squeezed into it without a false geography. Decision: the P6 town stays a compressed fictional neighbourhood beside "a" main road. The market and a warkop are represented because they are everyday neighbourhood types along that road (evidence above). The mosque, SPBU, school and PAG housing are reserved for **connected districts** reached at natural road boundaries (see the roadmap).

## 8. Visual references and limits

- No public photos were downloaded or traced. Street-level imagery services were not available, so **no facade is copied** from a real building.
- **Architecture** follows typical regional types:
  - single-storey houses with pitched zinc or tile roofs and front yards with low fences;
  - two-storey ruko with a shop at ground level and windows above;
  - open-front warkop with a deep roof and tables;
  - market kiosks with awnings/tarps.
- This is a design generalisation, not a verified survey of specific buildings.
- **Logos and brands** (Indomaret, Mixue, Pertamina) remain text/colour TEMP with brand review pending.
